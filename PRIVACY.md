# Privacy

Pixel Picker collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere. It has no telemetry, no
analytics, no account and no network access of its own.

- **Screen.** When you start picking, Pixel Picker takes a screenshot of your monitors to show
  the frozen screen and the magnifier. The screenshot stays in memory: it is never written to
  disk nor sent anywhere, and it is discarded as soon as picking ends.
- **Clipboard.** Pixel Picker writes the picked color's code to the clipboard, when you pick a
  color or click a Copy button. It never reads the clipboard.
- **History and settings.** The last picked colors, the zoom and the grid setting are kept in
  memory only, and forgotten when the program closes.
- **Links.** The links at the foot of the window (ialto.com, the licence) open in your browser
  only when you click them.
